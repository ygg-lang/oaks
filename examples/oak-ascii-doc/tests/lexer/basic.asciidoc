== Formatting Basics

Bold *text*, italic _text_, monospace `text`.
Curved quotes: "`double`" and '`single`'.
Replacements: (C) (R) (TM) -- ... -> <- => <=

== Lists

.Unordered
* Level 1
** Level 2
*** Level 3
* Level 1 again

.Ordered
. Step 1
. Step 2
.. Step 2a
.. Step 2b
. Step 3

.Checklist
- [*] Checked
- [x] Also checked
- [ ] Not checked

.Description List
Term:: Definition
Term 2::
Definition 2
